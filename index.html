Case Study 01 • Luxury Diamond Jewellery

Aura Fine Jewels

Idea Behind This: Inspired by the precision-cut brilliance of natural diamonds and royal heritage, the identity uses deep navy blue and sharp geometric typography to project uncompromised luxury and timeless prestige. Every packaging dieline and billboard is crafted to treat fine diamond jewellery not merely as an ornament, but as an enduring heirloom.

Idea Behind This: Born from the warmth of golden-brown artisanal baking, the branding blends delicate hand-crafted monograms with modern packaging folds to evoke the comforting aroma of fresh morning bakes.
